#skyrsdr for openwifi-hw

## Introduction
[SkyrSDR](https://github.com/guoli11/) is a SDR hardware platform based on [xilinx zynq7020](https://www.xilinx.com/products/silicon-devices/soc/zynq-7000.html) and [adi ad936x](https://www.analog.com/en/products/ad9361.html). 

SkyrSDR is a lowcost SDR platform.Hareware feature is : ZYNQ 7Z020CLG400-2 ,512MB DDR3 memory fo PS, 1G Ethernet RJ45 for PS,USB OTG(act as USB host or USB SLAVE ), USB uarts for PS,on board USB to JTAG debuger,TF card , bootable QSPI FLASH ,40MHZ 0.5PPM TXCO,and also 6 IOs from PL bank
It could be used as a traditional SDR device such as PlutoSDR, and it also be used as hardware platform to support openwifi.
